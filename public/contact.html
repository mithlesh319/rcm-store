Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common queries

How long does delivery take?
▾

We offer Express Delivery (2–4 hours), Normal Delivery (2–3 business days), and free Store Pickup. Delivery times may vary slightly based on your location and order volume.

Can I return or exchange a product?
▾

Yes! We offer a 7-day easy return and exchange policy on most products. If you received a damaged, defective, or wrong product, contact us within 7 days of delivery and we'll arrange a free pickup.

How do I track my order?
▾

Once your order is placed, you'll receive an Order ID via email and SMS. You can use this ID on our Track Order page, or WhatsApp us your Order ID for a real-time update.

Are all products 100% genuine RCM products?
▾

Absolutely. We are an authorized RCM Store and every product we sell is sourced directly from RCM Business Pvt. Ltd. Every item comes with an original manufacturer seal and guarantee.

What payment methods do you accept?
▾

We accept UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking, Google Pay, PhonePe, and Cash on Delivery (COD). All online payments are secured via Razorpay payment gateway.

How can I become an RCM distributor?
▾

We'd love to have you on board! Fill in the contact form above and select the "Distributor" topic, or call us directly. Our team will guide you through the RCM distributor registration process.

Do you deliver to my city?
▾

We deliver across all major cities and towns in India. For remote areas, delivery may take 4–7 business days. Enter your pincode at checkout to confirm delivery availability for your location.

What should I do if my payment failed but amount was deducted?
▾

If your payment failed but the amount was deducted from your account, don't worry — it will be automatically refunded within 5–7 business days. Contact us immediately with your transaction ID and we'll expedite the process.
